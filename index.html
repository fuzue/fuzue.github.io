Scientific computing

Released
KinBiont.jl
An ecosystem of numerical methods for microbial kinetics data analysis - from preprocessing to result interpretation. Covers growth curve fitting, model selection, segmentation, and comparative analysis across experimental conditions. Developed with the Pinheiro Group at Human Technopole.
Julia Microbial growth Kinetics Model fitting
github.com/pinheiroGroup/KinBiont.jl → github.com/fuzue/pykinbiont → E. coli knockout growth atlas →
Released
BayesBiont.jl
The Bayesian companion to KinBiont.jl. Samples the full posterior with NUTS via Turing.jl, supports hierarchical pooling across replicates with a non-centered reparameterisation, and compares competing models with PSIS-LOO. For when calibrated uncertainty and principled model comparison matter.
Julia Bayesian Turing.jl KinBiont.jl PSIS-LOO

Human Technopole · Pinheiro Group · Milan
Bacterial growth instrumentation & bioinformatics
ChiBio hardware integration · flow cytometry pipelines · Nature Communications
Laboratory

Working with the Pinheiro Group at Human Technopole in Milan, we customised ChiBio bioreactor hardware to provide finer experimental control beyond what the standard firmware allows. We then built the daily-use interfaces for plate reader data analysis and automated flow cytometry gating pipelines, integrated into the Kinbiont framework.

The software work contributed directly to a peer-reviewed publication in Nature Communications - listed under Fuzue Tech as the affiliated company. This is the basis of the #5 ranking among Polish companies in the Nature Index, and a rare distinction for a company in any field.

Digi Sapiens · B2B contract
ML engineering & edtech platform development
App dev team lead · Android · data warehouse · speech-to-text alignment
ML / Data

As app development team lead at Digi Sapiens, work spanned the full technical stack: Android application development, data warehouse architecture, and a particularly demanding speech-to-text alignment problem - matching spoken audio to text transcripts at word and phoneme level for educational use.

The alignment problem is NP-complete in its general form. The work involved surveying recent literature and developing a practical solution that performed well within the constraints of a production platform.

Follow The Grant · Investigative data journalism
Civic data pipeline: research conflict-of-interest investigation
15M relationships analyzed · European Science Journalism Award 2021 · BuzzFeed News, Frankfurter Rundschau, Business Insider
Civic Tech

Built the data engineering pipeline behind an award-winning cross-border investigation into undisclosed conflicts of interest in academic research. The pipeline processed and cross-referenced 15 million potential researcher–industry relationships to identify systematic non-disclosure of pharmaceutical ties.

The investigation was published in BuzzFeed News, Frankfurter Rundschau, Business Insider, and CIVIO. The project received the European Science Journalism Award 2021 from the European Federation for Science Journalism.
